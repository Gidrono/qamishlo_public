06 - How a course is born

From curiosity to lesson one, then the rest in the background.

  1. 01

    Browse the catalog - or name any topic

    Start from what’s already loved, or describe a subject and Qamishlo will generate an 8-10 chapter high quality AI course. If we already have that topic, we open it instantly.

  2. 02

    Pitched to their age

    Pick kids or adults. Explanations fit the listener - so the same curiosity can land for a child on the drive home or for you on headphones.

  3. 03

    Listen. Catalog is free; generate uses a pack.

    Multi-chapter audio with a calm teaching voice. Lesson one starts when ready; the rest prepares while you listen.

  4. 04

    Ask anytime - share when you’re ready

    Interrupt mid-lesson for a contextual answer. When a course you made is finished, share it with your family circle in one tap.
